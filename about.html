ABOUT LIMINA

Supporting mothers through transition.

Limina was created from a desire to support mothers through the many stages of pregnancy, birth, postpartum, and motherhood.

WHY LIMINA

You deserve support, too.

Motherhood can bring enormous change. Limina exists to create space for mothers to feel supported, informed, and cared for throughout that transition.

The goal is not to tell you how to experience motherhood. It is to meet you where you are, listen to what you need, and provide compassionate non-medical support along the way.

THE APPROACH

Whole-journey support.

Limina is designed to grow alongside the needs of mothers—from preparation and education, through birth, and into postpartum life.

As Limina grows, additional services, education, and resources will be added to support more parts of the motherhood journey.
