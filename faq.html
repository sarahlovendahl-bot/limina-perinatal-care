FAQ

Questions are welcome.

A starting place for common questions about Limina and perinatal support.

What is a doula?

A doula provides non-medical emotional, physical, and informational support during pregnancy, birth, and/or postpartum.

What does non-medical support mean?

Limina's services are intended to complement, not replace, care from qualified medical professionals.
